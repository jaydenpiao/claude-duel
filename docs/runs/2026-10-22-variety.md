## Reading
- **Variety works.** Before, mini reused one design ("Recursive Digit Puzzle") for all 6 challenges in cm-4.
  Now every match has 5-6 distinct designs (e.g. vm-8: modular lock, maze walker, weighted checksums, register
  VM, modular sequence).
- **Forcing a theme hurt validity** (5/12): hard themes produced unsatisfiable checks and failing solvers.
  Making the theme a suggestion brought it back to 7/12 and then 9/18.
- **Captures nearly vanished** (2 in 42 challenges). The new designs are harder than the old reused one, and
  mini attackers rarely crack them even with 5 tries.
- **No match met the demo bar** (varied puzzles, both sides capture and defend, a multi-try capture).
  - Best varied match: `vm-8` (5/6 valid, 6 distinct designs, 5 defenses, no capture).
  - Best two-sided match: still `cm-4` (docs/runs/2026-10-22-validity.md), but its puzzles repeat.
- Next lever: tune hardness toward the attacker, e.g. `gate_mode = "score"` (#78), or give attackers more tries
  on round-1 challenges. Tracked in #95.
